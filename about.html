What we do for you

We manage the deal. All of it.

Buying emeralds across borders has a lot of moving parts. We take care of them, so you can focus on the jewellery.

  1. 01

    The brief

    Tell us what you need. Shape, size, colour, quantity, budget and deadline. We ask the right questions up front so nothing gets lost later.

  2. 02

    Sourcing

    We buy directly at the mines of Muzo, Chivor and Coscuez in Boyacá. Single stones or full parcels. Rough or cut.

  3. 03

    Selection & grading

    Every stone is checked by our own team. You see photos of the actual stones before you commit.

  4. 04

    Cutting & matching

    Our cutters finish and calibrate each stone to your brief. Melee is matched for colour across the whole parcel.

  5. 05

    Certification

    Independent laboratory reports from CDTEC in Bogotá. Reports from GIA and other laboratories on request.

  6. 06

    Sale & delivery

    Sales are ex-works from wherever the stones are held. We prepare the invoice and sale documents, then assist with shipping and tracking.
