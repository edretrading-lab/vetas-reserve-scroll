01

Frequently asked questions.

Are your emeralds natural?

Yes. Every stone we offer is a natural emerald, formed in the ground, never laboratory-grown or synthetic. Anything other than a natural emerald would be stated plainly.

Where do your emeralds come from?

We source from three of Colombia’s finest mining regions: Muzo, Chivor and Coscuez, all in the Boyacá department. Each mine produces stones with distinct character; we select only the finest from each.

Are your emeralds treated?

As with almost all emeralds, most of ours have had their natural surface-reaching fractures filled to improve clarity, a long-standing and accepted practice. We disclose the degree of any treatment and wherever possible it is confirmed on an independent laboratory report.

Do stones come with certification?

Stones and parcels of consequence are accompanied by documentation from an independent gemmological laboratory confirming that the stone is a natural emerald, its treatment level, and, where the laboratory can determine it, its origin. Ask us what accompanies a particular stone.

How do I buy or reserve a stone?

We currently work by enquiry rather than online checkout. Tell us what you are looking for and we will send what is available, with certification and price. Terms are confirmed in writing before any payment is made.

Do you supply the trade, melee and parcels?

Yes. Alongside individual stones, we supply colour-matched melee and rough parcels to designers, manufacturers and houses.

How is pricing determined?

Emerald is priced on colour first, then clarity, cut and carat, together with origin and treatment level. Because fine single-origin material is scarce, price per carat rises steeply with quality and size. Prices are quoted per stone on enquiry.

How does delivery work?

Our sales are ex-works (EXW) from wherever the stones are held at the time of sale. The location is stated on your invoice. The stones are made available to you for collection once the sale is complete. We are happy to help arrange secure, insured, tracked courier shipping on your behalf. We will keep you updated until the stones arrive.

What about customs, duties and VAT?

Import duties, VAT or GST and any clearance fees are set by the destination country and are the recipient’s responsibility. We will help you understand what may apply, but cannot assume these charges.

Do you accept returns?

Return terms are agreed in writing before purchase and depend on the item. A stone that has been set, altered or damaged cannot be returned. Ask us about the terms for a specific stone before you commit.

How should I care for an emerald?

Gently, warm soapy water and a soft brush, never ultrasonic or steam cleaning and kept away from heat and chemicals.

Can I view a stone before buying?

Private viewings are arranged by appointment and by introduction. Enquire to arrange one.
